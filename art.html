Art

I’m a musician with 15 years of classical singing and piano experience. I truly believe in the healing power of music in bringing people together. I taught piano to visually impaired kids and organized concerts at senior livings, and these experiences changed my life in how I express myself through music and my understanding in music-making.

I also enjoy taking photos! I like capturing small moments happening in this world through my camera, and it reminds me to cherish every moment in my life and what we have now because it is purely beautiful.
